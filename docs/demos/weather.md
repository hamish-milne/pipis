# Weather

Finds your location and fetches the local weather when it loads, so the browser asks for permission to use your location. It shows [`Suspense`](../reference/api.md#suspense) for the pending, success and error states around a promise, [`Watch`](../reference/api.md#watch) for saving a preference to storage as a side effect, and reactive `data-*` attributes driving Tailwind's variants for the theme switch.
